Skeleton Notes is a simple, lightweight note taking app. No clutter, no bloat.

Features:
* Rich text formatting: bold, italic, strikethrough and underline
* Checklists
* Attach images and files to your notes
* Optional Nextcloud sync over WebDAV (self-hosted, your data stays yours)
* Import and export all your notes as a zip backup
* APK is 600KB

Free and open source (GPLv3). Runs on Android 7.0 and newer.
